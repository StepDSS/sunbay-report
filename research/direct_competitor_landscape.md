# Direct Competitor Landscape — Sunbay.io

**Scope:** Companies whose products include AR collections automation features targeting B2B SMBs, evaluated from two angles: (A) international players accessible to Polish SMBs, and (B) Polish-market incumbents. Sunbay included as baseline.

**Geography:** Poland (primary), with EU/global context for international players.

**Definition of "direct competitor":** Any company a Sunbay prospect might evaluate as an alternative — whether AR collections is their core product or a module within a broader platform.

## Part C — Synthesis

### What Sunbay Has That No One Else Does (in Poland)

1. **Polish invoicing system integrations + standalone AR automation as a SaaS product.** No international player has any Polish integrations. No Polish player offers a standalone, purpose-built AR automation product with workflow builder, multi-channel outreach, analytics dashboard, and branded templates — all in one.

2. **Proactive collections positioning with modern SaaS UX.** Vindicat is positioned as debt recovery (reactive). Comarch is an ERP module (locked-in). wFirma is a basic feature. Sunbay occupies the "proactive AR automation" niche that Chaser/Upflow occupy internationally, but localized for Poland.

### What Competitors Have That Sunbay Doesn't

1. **Vindicat:** BIG InfoMonitor integration, debt marketplace, e-court filing, legal support, 10 years of track record, 20K users, and a success fee model that removes upfront cost risk for the buyer.

2. **Chaser:** Payment portal, auto-calls (live, not "launching soon"), credit checks, AI features (live), 10K users, integrations with 15+ accounting/ERP systems, and $3.3M ARR proving the model works.

3. **Comarch Optima:** 60K captive users, sales blocking on overdue clients, native ERP integration (no data sync needed), and the backing of a publicly-listed company with ~€420M revenue.

4. **wFirma:** 49 PLN/mo price point that makes Sunbay look expensive for basic needs. "Good enough" for the bottom of the SMB market.

5. **All international players:** Payment portals, cash application, dispute management, multi-entity support, public APIs — features Sunbay hasn't built yet.

### The Gap Sunbay Sits In

Sunbay occupies a real gap: **purpose-built AR automation for Polish B2B SMBs who use local invoicing tools and want to get paid faster without switching to Comarch or buying enterprise software.** This gap exists because:

- International players (Chaser, Upflow, etc.) don't integrate with Polish accounting tools
- Vindicat is positioned as debt recovery, not proactive AR management
- wFirma/Fakturownia reminders are too basic for companies with 100+ invoices/month
- Comarch locks you into an entire ERP

The question is whether this gap is large enough and monetizable enough at €100/mo to support a VC-scale business. That is a market sizing question, not a competitive landscape question.
