### 2.4 FIBEN balance-sheet data (Banque de France, 2023)

- Average supplier payment period (hors micro): 51 days of purchases, stable vs 2022.
- 71% of PME (hors micro) paid suppliers within 60 days in 2023 (up from 70% in 2022). ~29% still pay late.
- Only 50% of grandes entreprises paid within 60 days.
- Transfer simulation: if all enterprises paid within 60 days, PMEs would gain €15B in cash flow (€4B for micro, €11B for PME hors micro). This cash would come primarily from grandes entreprises (−€15B) and other economic agents (−€4B).

[Source: Banque de France, base FIBEN (data to August 2024)]

### 2.5 Wholesale trade — late payment deterioration

- 61% of wholesale trade enterprises reported an increase in late payments in 2024 (up from 54% in 2023).
- Over 60% of payment delays in wholesale exceed 11 days.
- The deterioration is attributed to financial difficulties of client enterprises, declining activity in construction (−6.5%) and manufactured products (−2.5%).

[Source: CGF (Confédération des Grossistes de France) annual survey, cited in Observatoire 2024]

### 2.6 DGCCRF enforcement

- 737 establishments controlled in 2024, 42.3% anomaly rate (up from 34.3%).
- €69 million in total fines, up 18.5% YoY.
- Top sector by fine value: wholesale trade (including pharma) — €8M, 33 fines.
- The DGCCRF found that the majority of late payments stem from deficient internal organization: excessively long validation circuits, shared service centers abroad, and poor invoice formalism.

[Source: Banque de France, Observatoire des délais de paiement 2024, Section 3.1]

### 2.7 France mandatory B2B e-invoicing

France has one of the EU's most comprehensive B2B e-invoicing mandates:

- **Timeline:** September 2026 for large/mid-sized companies (send + receive); September 2027 for SMEs and micro-enterprises (send). All companies must be able to receive from September 2026.
- **Platform:** PPF (Portail Public de Facturation) + Plateformes Agréées (PA) in a 5-corner model. 106 approved platforms as of February 2026.
- **Pilot:** launched February 24, 2026 with 60,000 businesses out of 600,000 listed in the directory.
- **Formats:** UBL 2.1, UN/CEFACT CII, Factur-X (EN 16931-compliant).
- **Invoice lifecycle tracking:** mandatory statuses include submitted, refused, rejected, received. Payment status reporting is required for services.
- **What it does NOT do:** the mandate handles issuance, routing, and status tracking. It does not automate collections — no reminders, no escalation workflows, no payment matching. The mandate makes late invoices more visible without solving the collection problem.

[Source: EY Tax Alert, August 2025; Service-Public.fr; Basware; EDICOM]

| ERP | 48.9% | 41.1% | 80.1% | 69.9% |

France is above the EU average on ERP and CRM adoption. The 10–49 band shows a major ERP acceleration: 34% → 49% between 2023 and 2025. The BI gap (France below EU) indicates SMEs are underserved by analytics tools.

~45% of French small enterprises (10–49) still use no business software at all.

### 3.3 Accounting/invoicing software landscape in France

Leading domestic vendors for SMEs:
- **Sage** — dominant in French SME accounting/ERP
- **Cegid** — mid-market ERP + accounting
- **EBP** — micro/small enterprise accounting
- **Pennylane** — cloud-native, growing with accountant network
- **Xero / QuickBooks** — present but smaller market share than Sage/Cegid

None of these tools offer automated collections (payment reminder workflows, escalation cadences, multi-channel campaigns, payment matching) as a core feature. They issue invoices and record payments. Some have basic overdue flags. The gap between invoicing (issuing) and collections (getting paid) is the core of the market opportunity.

### 4.5 Data gaps

1. **ARPC validation against competitors.** Competitor pricing (Tesorio, Upflow, Flowie, Agicap Collections) ranges €200–500/month for SMEs, implying ARPC of €2,400–6,000. Using competitor pricing instead of Sunbay's current pricing would 2–5x the revenue figure. The €127.5M conservative TAM is a floor based on Sunbay's current (low) pricing.

2. **Accommodation (I) B2B portion.** Hotels have non-trivial B2B segments (corporate travel, events). If quantifiable, some of I's 20,409 enterprises could be added to extended TAM. Currently excluded.

3. **Adoption rate / SOM.** The TAM is at 100% penetration. No adoption rate applied. The Eurostat data showing 45% of 10–49 French enterprises use no business software at all is both a barrier and a whitespace indicator.

4. **G45/G46/G47 split updated to 2023.** The France-specific ratios are from 2020. The structural composition likely shifted since then (e-commerce growth in retail, consolidation in wholesale). A 2023 split would be more accurate but is not available at division level in the `sbs_sc_ovw` table.
