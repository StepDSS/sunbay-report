# Team Analysis — Sunbay.io

## Founders

### Filip Szczeciński — CEO (GTM, Product Strategy, Investors)
- Full-time since May 2025. Based in Kraków.
- Background: ~9 years in operations roles across Polish tech companies (HCM Deck, CallPage, JIVR) and one YC-backed company (Plane, W17) where he was Business Operations Manager for 2+ years. Also ran an ops consulting side practice (Exciting Ops) for ~2 years.
- Education: Economics, Jagiellonian University (no graduate degree listed).
- The deck describes him as responsible for GTM. His career history contains zero sales or GTM titles — his experience is operations and process management. The GTM responsibility is aspirational relative to his track record.
- He was an **employee** at Plane, not a founder. The deck's phrasing ("previously YC-backed Plane") is factually accurate but could be misread as implying founder involvement.

### Dawid Dzierzyński — COO / CRO (Customer Success, Product Delivery, Operations & Finance)
- Full-time since April 2025. Based in Warsaw.
- Background: Finance Director at Droppe (VC-backed marketplace, Helsinki, 1 year), Finance and Business Ops Manager at HCM Deck (VC-backed HR-tech, 3 years, managing a team of 3). Prior to that, student leadership roles at UEK Kraków.
- Education: Master's in Finance and Accounting, Kozminski University; Bachelor's in Economics with Financial Analytics, UEK Kraków.
- **Title discrepancy:** LinkedIn says "CRO & Co-founder." The pitch deck says "COO." These are different roles — CRO implies revenue/sales ownership, COO implies operations. His actual background is finance operations, not revenue generation.
- His experience at Droppe and HCM Deck gives him direct exposure to VC-backed startup finance and operations, which is relevant to Sunbay's stage.
- Languages: English (professional), German (limited working).

## Network & Client Acquisition

- The ~10 clients on the website are overwhelmingly Kraków-based Polish SMBs, with two nonprofits. The cluster pattern and company profiles indicate founder-network-driven acquisition, not scalable GTM.
- Both angel investors (Szymon Brodziak of S16VC, Sebastian Bobrowski co-founder of inFakt) are Polish and appear to come from founder/ops community connections.
- This demonstrates a functional local network but provides no evidence of ability to sell outside personal connections or outside Poland.

## Gaps

1. **No sales or GTM experience on the founding team.** Filip is ops, Dawid is finance, Nikodem is engineering. The GTM hire is a university student. For a company that needs to sell to SMB finance teams across Europe, this is a structural gap.
2. **Solo engineer building the product.** Nikodem is the only technical person mentioned. No other developers, no contractor references. Scaling the product and maintaining it with one mid-level engineer is a constraint.
3. **First-time founders across the board.** None of the three have previously founded or co-founded a company. All prior roles were employee positions.
4. **No advisory board mentioned.** No advisors listed in the deck or on the website. The investors (Brodziak, Bobrowski) could serve advisory functions, but this is not stated.

## What Holds

- The team has clear role separation: business/ops (Filip), finance/operations (Dawid), engineering (Nikodem). There is minimal overlap in their functional responsibilities.
- Prior working relationships (Filip–Dawid at HCM Deck) reduce co-founder conflict risk relative to teams that met specifically to start a company.
- Dawid's direct experience in VC-backed startup finance (Droppe, HCM Deck) is directly applicable to managing Sunbay's own finances and understanding the investor relationship.
- Nikodem's mBank/BLIK experience with payment microservices is directly relevant to building payment-adjacent infrastructure.
