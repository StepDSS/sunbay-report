### KSeF — Mandatory B2B E-Invoicing

Source: Polish law signed August 27, 2025. Quality: **[A]**.

Timeline: Feb 1, 2026 mandatory for large taxpayers (turnover >PLN 200M). **April 1, 2026 mandatory for ALL other VAT-registered businesses including SMEs.** Jan 1, 2027 micro-entrepreneurs. Grace period throughout 2026, full enforcement with penalties Jan 1, 2027.

What KSeF does: centralized clearance platform; validates and assigns unique KSeF ID to every B2B invoice; archives 10 years; FA(3) XML format.

What KSeF does NOT do: does not track whether invoices are paid. No payment matching, no reminders, no overdue data.

TAM implication: From April 2026, every Polish SME will be digitally issuing invoices through KSeF but will still be manually chasing payments. KSeF creates a structured data layer (standardized XML with unique IDs) that makes it technically easier for AR automation tools to ingest invoice data.

## 6. Data Gaps

1. **No adoption rate applied.** TAM counts all firms in scope, not just those likely to buy. A realistic SAM would apply a penetration ceiling (e.g., firms with 50+ invoices/month, or firms reporting late payment issues — 72% per EU Payment Observatory 2024).
2. **ARPC uses list pricing midpoint.** Custom tier (3000+ invoices) is "contact us" and likely higher. Blended ARPC depends on customer mix.
3. **No data on invoice volume by firm size.** Firms with few invoices/month may not need automation.
4. **Consulting revenue excluded.** Sunbay currently earns ~€3K/month from consulting alongside €1.2–1.5K SaaS.
5. **G45 B2B split (50%) is an estimate.** Could be validated with NACE 4-digit data from GUS BDL.
6. **J and L B2B splits (75%, 50%) are estimates.** Could be improved with GUS revenue-by-client-type data.
7. **Sections I, P, Q, R, S excluded from both TAMs.** Some Q (private clinics) and P (training companies) do B2B invoicing.
