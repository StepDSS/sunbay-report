## Check Size Analysis

S16VC does not publicly disclose individual check sizes. Here is what can be inferred:

### Fund I (size undisclosed, ~35 deals)
- Fund I was smaller than Fund II. If total AUM is >$100M and Fund II is $70M, Fund I was likely in the $30–40M range.
- With ~35 deals, the average initial check was likely **$500K–$1M** range.
- Many Fund I deals appear to have been smaller angel-style checks, consistent with S16VC's origin as a "founder-centric angel fund" (Crunchbase description).
- Synthesia and Oyster, the breakout successes, were likely small early checks that S16VC may or may not have followed on (unclear from public data).

### Fund II ($70M, ~15 deals as of late 2024)
- Average allocation per deal: $70M ÷ 15 = ~$4.7M if evenly distributed. But: funds reserve capital for follow-ons (typically 30–50% of fund), so initial checks are smaller.
- Assuming 50% reserves: $35M deployed across 15 initial checks = ~$2.3M average initial check.
- Assuming 30% reserves and ~25 target total deals (new + follow-on): initial checks likely **$1–3M range**.

### Evidence from specific deals:
- **TaxNova** (Pre-Seed, $1M total round): S16VC was one of 3 named institutional backers + 20 angels. S16VC's check was likely **$100K–$300K** — this is a pre-seed from Fund II, very small.
- **DubFormer** (Seed, $3.6M total): Almaz Capital led. S16VC was a named co-investor alongside FinSight + angels. Check likely **$300K–$800K**.
- **Stacks** ($3M pre-seed + $7M seed = $10M): EQT led pre-seed, General Catalyst led seed. S16VC participated. Check likely **$500K–$1.5M** across both rounds.
- **Wordsmith** (Seed, $5M): Index Ventures led, General Catalyst, Felix, Yellow, and S16VC participated. S16VC's check likely **$300K–$700K**.
- **Theker AI** (Seed, €3M): JME led, S16VC, Mission VC, Itnig participated. Check likely **$300K–$600K**.
- **Playgama** (Seed, $3M): Co-led by TOP and S16VC. As co-lead, check was likely **$500K–$1M**.
- **Cynomi** (Seed $3.5M, 2022): S16VC was named investor. Check likely **$300K–$700K** at seed, with follow-on in Series A and B.
- **Newo.ai** (Series A, $25M): Led by Ratmir Timashev. S16VC was one of 4 named participants. Check likely **$1–3M**.

### Pattern summary:
- When S16VC **leads or co-leads** a seed: $500K–$1.5M
- When S16VC **participates** in a seed alongside a lead: $200K–$800K
- When S16VC participates in a **pre-seed**: $100K–$300K
- When S16VC participates in a **Series A**: $1–3M (follow-on or new entry)
- **Series B follow-ons** (Cynomi): check size unknown but likely $500K–$2M as a smaller follow-on investor

### The Sunbay deal in context:
The brief states $1.2M at $6M post-money. This would be a **20% ownership stake**, which is large for a seed round. If S16VC were writing a $1.2M check:
- This is on the high end of their typical seed range
- It would represent ~1.7% of Fund II capital in a single deal
- At 20% ownership, S16VC would be the lead or sole institutional investor — not a co-investor riding alongside Index or General Catalyst
- This pattern is more consistent with deals like Playgama ($3M seed, co-led by S16VC) than with deals where S16VC sits alongside a Tier 1 lead

**Key note from memory:** Sunbay has raised $60K so far in an Angel round. The $1.2M at $6M post-money is the **target** raise, not what has closed. Szymon Brodziak (listed on Crunchbase as S16VC's Head of Investment Analytics) invested personally, not through the fund.
