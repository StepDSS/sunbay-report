# Traction & Benchmarking

## 1. Sunbay's Traction — What We Know

**Hard data (from pitch deck, April 2025):**
- MRR: $1.2k–$1.5k from SaaS invoicing (5–6 paying clients)
- Consulting revenue: ~$3k referenced alongside April 2025 traction data. Time period, recurrence, and scope of work are unspecified. [Source: Sunbay Pitch Deck, slide 10]
- North star metric: 1,981 total automated actions (Jan–Apr 2025 cumulative)
- Modules active: CollectionModule (dominant), DocumentFlowModule, InvoicingModule
- CollectionModule usage grew from 254 actions (Jan 2025) to 638 actions (Apr 2025) [Source: Sunbay Pitch Deck, slide 10]

**Estimated data (March 2026):**
- Website displays 10 client logos: Tequipy, Grail Point, Trify, Feelgood, PAGA, Kuek, ISO Serious, Everuptive, Beeffective, Flowmore [Source: sunbay.io, accessed 2026-03-18]
- Pitch video claims 20 customers (unverified, contradicts both deck and website) [Source: user notes from pitch video]
- Assuming ~10 paying customers at stable ACV (€100–120/mo), estimated MRR is ~$2.4k–$3k, or ~$29k–$36k ARR
- Caveats: not all logos are necessarily paying customers. Two are nonprofits (PAGA, Kuek). Some could be pilots, free trials, or consulting-only relationships. No data on tier mix (Basic vs. Standard vs. Custom).

**Missing data that would normally be expected:**
- Current MRR (March 2026) — not provided
- Month-over-month revenue growth rate — cannot calculate with one snapshot
- Churn / retention — zero data
- Net revenue retention (NRR) — no data
- CAC and payback period — no data
- Pipeline or conversion rates — no data
- ACV distribution across tiers — no data

## 2. Benchmarking Against Seed-Stage SaaS

| Metric | Sunbay (est. Mar 2026) | Seed-stage B2B SaaS benchmarks |
|---|---|---|
| MRR | ~$2.4k–$3k | $20k–50k+ typical at seed |
| ARR | ~$29k–$36k | $150k–500k for top-tier seed raises |
| Paying customers | ~10 | 10–50+ |
| ACV | ~€1,300/yr (~$1,400) | $5k–25k+ for B2B SaaS |
| MoM growth | Unknown | 15–20% target for post-seed |
| Monthly churn | Unknown | 3.5% median for B2B SaaS |
| Runway | ~8–12 months ($60K raised) | 24–30 months expected by investors |

Sunbay's customer count (~10) is approaching the low end of seed-stage range. Revenue is not, because ACV is very low. At €100–120/mo, Sunbay would need ~700–830 paying customers to reach €1M ARR. That requires a high-volume SMB sales motion.

The $6M post-money valuation on ~$18K ARR implies a revenue multiple of ~333x. This is not unusual for pre-seed/angel rounds where the investment thesis is team and market, not current revenue — but it means the valuation is entirely forward-looking.

**Sources for benchmarks:**
- Top-tier seed companies at $150K–$500K ARR raising $2–4M at $20–25M post-money [Source: https://www.flowjam.com/blog/seed-round-valuation-2025-complete-founders-guide]
- 15–20% MoM MRR growth as target for post-seed SaaS [Source: https://tomtunguz.com/mrr-growth/]
- Median B2B SaaS churn rate 3.5% [Source: Recurly 2025 Churn Report, via https://www.vitally.io/post/saas-churn-benchmarks, April 2025]
- 24–30 months runway expected [Source: https://www.forumvc.com/thought-pieces/raise-for-resilience, 2025]
- Early-stage ($1–10M ARR) benchmarks: 80–120% ARR growth, NRR 100–110%, CAC payback 18–24 months [Source: https://www.re-cap.com/benchmarking-tool]

## 4. Upflow's Trajectory Post-Seed — Category Ceiling Signal

Upflow's post-seed trajectory provides data on what happens in this category even with structural advantages:

- **Jun 2021 — Series A ($15M):** By this point, Upflow was processing $200M+ in invoices monthly for 1.5M transacting companies. Named clients: Lattice, Front, Adikteev. Planned to triple team size. Opened NYC office. [Source: https://medium.com/inside-hexa/upflow-ef18-grabs-15m-series-a-to-revolutionize-how-b2b-businesses-get-paid-fce391e09ce6]
- **Apr 2024 — Series A extension ($5M):** Funding for "Payments by Upflow" — a pivot into payment processing layered on top of the AR SaaS. Led by Lorimer Ventures. [Source: https://upflow.io/blog/business-to-business-payments/upflow-announces-5-million-in-new-funding-to-revolutionize-b2b-payments, April 2024]
- **Current (2025/2026):** 68–75 employees. Revenue estimated at $4.1M (Getlatka, unverified). Total funding: $22.9–25.8M across 5 rounds. [Source: https://getlatka.com/companies/upflow; https://tracxn.com/d/companies/upflow/; https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/268120-18]

The $5M extension in 2024 — three years after a $15M Series A — to fund a payments pivot suggests the core AR SaaS business alone did not generate sufficient growth to raise a traditional Series B.

## 6. Key Takeaways for the Memo

**On Sunbay's traction specifically:**
- Sunbay's traction is pre-seed level by US/EU benchmarks. 5–10 customers at €100–120/mo is early signal, not product-market fit.
- The consulting revenue (~$3k, unspecified period and scope) is not classifiable as recurring product revenue without further detail.
- Customer growth from 5–6 to ~10 over ~11 months is movement, but without MRR time series, churn data, or NRR, it is not possible to determine whether growth is accelerating, linear, or decelerating.

**On the category, using comps:**
- Upflow had ~$742K revenue, 18 employees, eFounders studio backing, and Kima Ventures at its $2.76M seed. Seven years and $23M+ later, it is at an estimated ~$4M revenue and pivoted into payments.
- Chaser has been in market 12 years with $5–6M total funding, 33 employees, and no new raise since 2019.
- Neither company has produced a venture-scale outcome from AR automation SaaS alone. Upflow's payments pivot suggests the pure AR SaaS model may have a revenue ceiling.
- For S16VC ($70M fund), the question is whether Sunbay can reach a scale that neither of these better-resourced competitors achieved. The comp data does not support that thesis on its own.
