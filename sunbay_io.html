THE PROBLEM

After the sale, revenue still depends
on manual work

After a deal is closed, revenue still depends on dozens of small, manual steps across tools.
When execution relies on memory, cash collection becomes slow, inconsistent, and hard to scale.

①

invoices get delayed because data sits in emails/CRM/contracts

②

follow-ups happen too late, too randomly, or not at all

③

payment status is checked manually across banking/invoicing tools

④

exceptions pile up and steal senior finance time

THE SOLUTION

Sunbay takes responsibility for revenue after the sale - so your team doesn’t have to.

Sunbay runs invoicing, follow-ups, and payment tracking automatically
- across the tools you already use.

PERSONALIZED:

Tailor your collections messaging with branded templates across Email and SMS

Personalize content using invoice data (customer name, due date, outstanding balance) and tiered reminder cadences (first reminder, second notice, escalation).

Save presets for different customer segments and channel preferences, and preview how each message will look before sending.

CUSTOMIZED:

Define a custom collection workflow with multi-channel automation (Email, SMS)

Configure rules: time-to-first-reminder, days past due thresholds, and automatic escalations .

Enable real-time open/read tracking, interest notes, and automatic generation of late-payment documents (interest notices, settlement proposals, or legal documents) with one-click sharing to customers.

DATA-DRIVEN:

Monitor collection performance with a comprehensive dashboard

See top debtors , open rates, and forecast cash flow to proactively shift collections strategies.
